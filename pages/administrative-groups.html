Erase / Archive Student —

Where would you like to erase or archive this student?

Archive Student (Preserve History)
Hides this student from the active list while preserving all their infractions, notes, and action timeline safely in archives.
Erase only from Administrative Groups
Removes student demographics, discipline records, and follow-ups from Administrative Groups. The student remains in Group Editor and class rosters.
Erase from everywhere (Administrative Groups & Group Editor)
Permanently deletes this student from Administrative Groups, the master student roster, and all classes in Group Editor.

Saves a complete timestamped backup snapshot of all student records and actions to the archive before clearing them from active view.
Erase all only from Administrative Groups
Clears all administrative records, infractions, and action logs. Group Editor and class rosters remain completely untouched.
Erase all from everywhere (Admin Groups & Group Editor)
Permanently deletes all students from Administrative Groups, the master student roster, and all class groups in Group Editor.
