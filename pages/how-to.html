Window Reorder Helper (Ctrl+Shift+W) & Quick Switcher (Ctrl+K)

Press Ctrl+K from any tool window to open the Quick Switcher and jump directly to tools, classes, students, or recent documents. Press Ctrl+Shift+W (or open Reorder Windows from the menu, Quick Switcher, or Board / Class Management presentation bars) to drag-reorder open windows, tile them (50/50, 20/80, 80/20, Grid, Stack), swap Main View and Mirrored View across displays, or save and apply custom app-and-size presets.
